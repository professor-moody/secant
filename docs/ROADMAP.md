# Secant Roadmap

This roadmap is organized in three horizons, plus the `2026-11-10` strategic positioning and academic/ecosystem milestones below. **Horizon 1** closes every experimental and implementation caveat documented in the v0.2.0 technical paper before any new surface area is added.

## Strategic Scope & Open-Source Release Boundary (`2026-11-10` Amendment)

- **Own the Quantized Value Layer:** Secant owns static, pre-load **value-level** auditing of floating-point and quantized weight files across all 10 GGUF block formats (`Q4_0`, `Q4_1`, `Q5_0`, `Q5_1`, `Q8_0`, `Q8_1`, `Q2_K`, `Q3_K`, `Q4_K`, `Q5_K`, `Q6_K`, `Q8_K` / K-quants & IQ grids), SafeTensors (extending to block-quant `GPTQ`/`AWQ`/`FP8`/sub-byte layouts), PEFT LoRA adapters (`adapter_model.safetensors`), and upcoming MLX weight layouts:
  - Block scales, K-quant/IQ zero scales, scale magnitudes, `qs`/`qh`/sign/grid-index bounds, `NVFP4`/`MXFP4` scale checks, MoE routing indices, `Rule S-0` companion-config bounds, and rank-one/rank-$k$ spectral and reference-diff edits (`secant audit-trojan --rank-k`, `--reference`), paired with a `llama.cpp --check-tensors` differential `FACTS` sheet.
  - Container/header framing fuzzing (ONNX, pickle, TensorRT, GGUF header truncation) stays in Crucible (`native/crucible-mutator-rs`); Crucible's Go `internal/wsp` remains the independent byte-exact parity oracle (scheduled to fold `TG-SECANT-001` F16 subnormal decode and `TG-SECANT-003` unfused `float64` rounding fencing across `Dot`, `GramSchmidt`, `compose`, `patch`, and `heal`).
- **`v0.3.0` Release Hygiene & Ecosystem Seams (30-90d, Prerequisite to External Outreach):**
  - Tag and publish the `v0.3.0` GitHub Release (bridging the gap between `CHANGELOG.md` `[0.3.0]` and GitHub's `v0.2.0` tag) with `dist` archives, `cosign` signatures, `SECURITY.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md` (including explicit AI-contribution policy), `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, and documented `0 / 1 / 2` CLI exit codes in `README.md`.
  - Ship zero-friction distribution and CI/runtime seams: Homebrew tap, manual initial crates.io publish + `maturin` `bin` PyPI wheels (`uvx secant`), SARIF 2.1.0 output (`partialFingerprints.primaryLocationLineHash` + `automationDetails.id`), CycloneDX 1.6 ML-BOM and Project Oak-pattern `in-toto` claim Statements, GitHub Action (`action.yml`) + `.pre-commit-hooks.yaml`, and an out-of-tree `vllm` `register_model_loader("secant")` plugin verified on a local GPU workstation before January 2027 `security@huggingface.co` scanner-partner outreach (all external outreach gated on written employer outside-activity / IP preclearance).

## Deferred / Dropped Until Requested

- **Multi-input calibration:** Deferred. The 7-point dose ramp on `Qwen3.5-4B` shows that single-reference calibration behaves predictably across dose (`0.50x..1.50x`).
- **`Q4_K` (and `Q6_K`, `TG-SECANT-012`) superblock editing:** Dropped (`secant quant patch` keeps the explicit refusal). The 32-weight `Q8_0` path covers demonstrated in-place GGUF surgery cleanly.
- **Additional single-file Trojan grid cells:** Not planned. The 17-cell, 187-point study answered the single-file question; reference diffing (`W - W_ref`) is the next step.
- **Structural pickle / ONNX / TensorRT container scanning:** Out of scope / dropped for Secant. Structural format framing and deserialization fuzzing belong to Crucible; Secant stays focused on weight-space and companion-config value invariants.

## Standing Evidence & Versioning Rules

- **Manifest before prose:** Every new number enters a `data/<study>/` directory with a verified `MANIFEST.sha256` (`shasum -a 256 -c MANIFEST.sha256` exiting `0` on the final committed files) before it enters a sentence in `docs/` or `README.md`.
- **Frozen `v0.3.0` paper body:** Once Horizon 1 Item 1 closes across all 6 RMS models and `v0.3.0` is tagged, the body of `docs/PAPER.md` is frozen for citation stability; subsequent Horizon 2 results enter a dated appendix or addendum rather than mutating existing body sections.
- **Mandatory Oracle Preflight & Benign FPR Gate (Crucible Rules 9 & 10 parity):** Every new `secant sanitize` or `secant audit-trojan` rule must (1) pass preflight against a planted or known historical violation (recording `PASS` or `BLIND` explicitly; silence is never a label) and (2) record its measured false-positive rate on the clean checkpoint corpus (`0%` or an explicitly bounded threshold) before gating CI or naming any discrepancy.
